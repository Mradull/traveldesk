HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT
Every rule has an ID in square brackets, for example [POL-6.2]. The TravelDesk
assistant "Vera" quotes these IDs when it answers policy questions, so you can look
the rule up yourself. The numeric limits in Sections 3, 4, 6 and 7 are also
configured in the TravelDesk backend. If Finance changes a number here, the same
number must be changed in the backend configuration so that the system and the
document never disagree.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1 - PURPOSE AND SCOPE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[POL-1.1] Purpose
This policy explains how employees request business travel, how travel requests are
approved and closed, how travel expenses are claimed, and which expense claims are
approved automatically and which need a manager's review.

[POL-1.2] Who this policy applies to
This policy applies to every employee of Veridian Global Services who travels for
business, and to every line manager who approves travel requests and expense claims.

[POL-1.3] What this policy does not cover
TravelDesk does not book flights or hotels, does not show live fares, does not issue
cash advances, does not settle foreign currency, and does not score travel safety.
Bookings are made through the approved travel agency; TravelDesk only records the
trip request and the expenses that result from it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2 - ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[POL-2.1] Employee
The employee submits a travel request before travelling, photographs the receipt for
every expense, raises expense tickets only while the trip is "Approved - Active",
and reads the manager's comment on any rejected ticket. Employees cannot approve
their own requests and cannot complete a trip.

[POL-2.2] Travel approver (line manager)
The line manager reviews the travel requests of their direct reports and approves or
rejects them, reviews every expense ticket that the system flags under Section 9,
records each decision with a review comment, and marks finished trips as completed.
Managers decide from the trip details, the receipt photos, the policy flags and the
decision history. Weather information and the assistant Vera are not part of the
manager's view.

[POL-2.3] TravelDesk system
TravelDesk applies the rules in this document in the same way every time. It checks
each expense line, records policy flags, routes each ticket either to automatic
approval or to the manager, completes trips automatically 90 days after their start
date, and keeps a permanent history of every decision.

[POL-2.4] Vera, the AI travel assistant
Vera is the AI chatbot inside TravelDesk. Vera can explain this policy, explain how
to use TravelDesk, and give a weather forecast and packing advice for a destination
and dates that you type into the chat. Vera cannot see your trips, tickets, expenses,
receipts or any other TravelDesk record. Vera's answers are informational only: Vera
cannot approve, reject, submit, edit or book anything and cannot promise an outcome.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3 - TRAVEL REQUESTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[POL-3.1] Information required in a travel request
Every travel request must include the destination city, the travel start date, the
travel end date and the business purpose. The employee's name and department are
taken from the employee's TravelDesk profile. When the request is saved, TravelDesk
gives it a trip ID such as TRP-00012.

[POL-3.2] Travel date rules
  - The start date must be today or a future date. It cannot be in the past.
  - The end date must be later than the start date.
  - A single trip can last at most 30 days, counting both the start and end dates.
  - A trip cannot start more than one year (365 days) from today.
A request that breaks any of these rules is not saved; TravelDesk shows the error
under the date field.

[POL-3.3] Destination rules
The destination must be a real city or town name of 2 to 100 characters, for example
"Mumbai" or "Singapore". Letters, spaces, commas, full stops, apostrophes, brackets
and hyphens are allowed.

[POL-3.4] Business purpose
The purpose must be 10 to 500 characters and must describe the business reason for
the trip, for example "Client workshop for the FY27 audit planning".

[POL-3.5] Editing a request before approval
While a request is "In Progress" (waiting for the manager), the employee can edit the
destination, dates and purpose. The same date rules apply. Once the manager has
approved or rejected the request, it can no longer be edited.

[POL-3.6] Manager decision on a travel request
The manager either approves or rejects the request. A rejection must include a review
comment of at least 10 characters explaining why. An approval comment is optional. An
approved trip becomes "Approved - Active" immediately. A manager cannot approve a
trip whose start date was more than 90 days ago.

[POL-3.7] Rejected requests
A rejected request is final and cannot be reopened. If the trip is still needed, the
employee submits a new travel request with the corrected details.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4 - TRIP STATUSES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[POL-4.1] Trip status list
  In Progress          : submitted, waiting for the manager's decision.
  Rejected             : the manager rejected the request. No expenses can be claimed.
  Approved - Active    : approved. Expense tickets can be raised.
  Approved - Completed : the trip is closed. No new tickets and no new expenses.

[POL-4.2] Active trips
Only trips that are "Approved - Active" accept new expense tickets. You can raise
tickets before the trip starts (for example for a flight booked in advance), during
the trip and after it, until the trip is completed.

[POL-4.3] How a trip becomes completed
A trip becomes "Approved - Completed" in one of two ways:
  a) your line manager marks it completed, on or after the trip end date, or
  b) TravelDesk completes it automatically 90 days after the trip start date.
Employees cannot change the status of a trip; there is no "complete" button for
employees. The trip page shows the automatic completion date from the day the trip is
created. Submit your claims before that date.

[POL-4.4] Completed trips
A completed trip cannot receive new tickets or new expenses and cannot be reopened.
Tickets submitted before completion are still reviewed by the manager as normal. The
trip page shows who completed the trip (the manager's name, or "System") and when.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5 - EXPENSE TICKETS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[POL-5.1] What an expense ticket is
An expense ticket is one claim submission for one trip. A ticket contains one or more
expense lines. Each expense line is one expense with its own expense ID and its own
receipt photo.

[POL-5.2] When a ticket can be raised
A ticket can be raised only for your own trip, and only while that trip is
"Approved - Active".

[POL-5.3] Information required for each expense line
Each expense line must have: the expense category (see [POL-6.1]), the amount in INR,
the date the expense was incurred (see Section 8), a short description of 3 to 300
characters, and a photo of the receipt (see [POL-7.1]). The invoice number printed on
the receipt is optional ([POL-7.2]).

[POL-5.4] Number of lines per ticket
A ticket must contain at least 1 and at most 20 expense lines.

[POL-5.5] Forgotten expenses
If you forget some expenses, raise another ticket for the same trip with the missing
expenses. A trip can have any number of tickets while it is "Approved - Active".

[POL-5.6] IDs shown in TravelDesk
Trips are shown as TRP-xxxxx, tickets as TKT-xxxxx, expense lines as EXP-xxxxx and
receipt photos (evidence) as EVD-xxxxx. Quote these IDs when contacting Finance.

[POL-5.7] Submitted tickets cannot be changed
A ticket cannot be edited, withdrawn or resubmitted after submission. Once a ticket is
approved, auto-approved or rejected, it is final and read-only, including its expense
lines, its receipt photos and the manager's decision and comment.

[POL-5.8] Correcting a rejected ticket
If a ticket is rejected, read the manager's reason and comment, then raise a NEW
ticket for the same trip while the trip is still "Approved - Active". The new ticket:
  - belongs to the same trip ID,
  - gets a new ticket ID and new expense IDs,
  - needs every receipt photo uploaded again; each upload gets a new evidence ID.
Nothing is copied from the rejected ticket. The new ticket is checked from the start
like any other ticket. The rejected ticket stays unchanged for audit. Each rejected
ticket can be replaced by one new ticket; TravelDesk links the two so you can see the
history.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 6 - EXPENSE CATEGORIES AND LIMITS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[POL-6.1] Allowed expense categories
Only these five categories are allowed:
  AIRFARE          : flights and flight changes (economy class).
  LODGING          : hotel or serviced-apartment stays.
  MEALS            : meals while travelling.
  GROUND_TRANSPORT : taxis, cabs, trains, buses, metro and airport transfers.
  INCIDENTALS      : small business costs such as laundry on trips longer than
                     4 days, printing, or local SIM cards.

[POL-6.2] Review thresholds per expense line
Any single expense line above its category threshold needs a manager's review.
  Category           Review threshold per line (INR)
  AIRFARE            20,000
  LODGING             7,500
  MEALS               1,500
  GROUND_TRANSPORT    3,000
  INCIDENTALS         1,000
An amount exactly equal to the threshold does not need review. An amount above the
threshold is not automatically rejected; it is sent to the manager with an
"Over threshold" flag.

[POL-6.3] Maximum amount per line
No single expense line can be more than INR 2,00,000. TravelDesk will not accept a
line above this amount. Split genuine large costs into separate lines (for example
one lodging line per hotel invoice), each with its own receipt photo.

[POL-6.4] Amount format
Amounts must be greater than zero, in INR, with at most two decimal places.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 7 - RECEIPT PHOTOS (EVIDENCE)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[POL-7.1] A receipt photo is required for every expense line
Every expense line needs a clear photo of its receipt, whatever the amount. A ticket
with any line that has no photo cannot be submitted. Accepted files: JPEG, PNG or
WEBP images up to 5 MB, one photo per line. The whole receipt must be readable,
including the amount, date and seller. Each uploaded photo gets an evidence ID.

[POL-7.2] Invoice number (optional)
You may also type the invoice or bill number printed on the receipt, 3 to 50
characters, using letters, numbers, hyphens, underscores or slashes. Examples:
"INV-88231", "IXR/2026/0045". It helps Finance and the duplicate check. Keep the
original receipt for audit.

[POL-7.3] Lost receipts
Expenses without a receipt photo cannot be claimed in TravelDesk. If a receipt is
lost, contact Finance Operations before raising a ticket.

[POL-7.4] Duplicate receipts
The same receipt cannot be claimed twice. If the same photo file, or the same invoice
number, was already used on another expense line of yours that was not rejected, the
new line is flagged "Duplicate receipt" and goes to the manager. A receipt photo from
a rejected ticket may be uploaded again for its replacement ticket ([POL-5.8]). One
uploaded photo can be attached to only one expense line.

[POL-7.5] Who can see receipt photos
Receipt photos are stored privately in TravelDesk. Only the employee who uploaded them
and the manager reviewing the ticket can open them. Cover full card numbers before you
take the photo.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 8 - EXPENSE DATES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[POL-8.1] Expense date cannot be in the future
The expense date is the date the money was spent. TravelDesk will not accept a
future expense date.

[POL-8.2] Expense date cannot be before the trip was created
An expense must be dated on or after the day its trip ID was created. Expenses from
before the trip request existed are rejected, and TravelDesk shows the error under
the expense date field ("Expense date is before this trip was created"). Expenses
dated before the trip starts (for example an advance flight booking) or during or
after the trip are accepted, as long as they are on or after the creation date and
the trip is still "Approved - Active".

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 9 - APPROVAL ROUTING (DETERMINISTIC RULES)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[POL-9.1] Policy flags
TravelDesk checks every expense line against these two rules:
  Flag code                  Rule                                           Policy
  OVER_THRESHOLD             Amount above the category threshold            [POL-6.2]
  DUPLICATE_RECEIPT          Same photo or invoice number already claimed   [POL-7.4]
Flags never block submission; they send the ticket to the manager. Problems that DO
block submission are shown as errors instead: a missing receipt photo ([POL-7.1]), an
expense date before the trip was created ([POL-8.2]) or in the future ([POL-8.1]),
and the amount and format rules in Section 6.

[POL-9.2] Automatic approval
If no line in a ticket has any flag, the whole ticket is approved automatically by
the system at submission. Its status becomes "Auto-approved" and the decision is
recorded with the reviewer "SYSTEM".

[POL-9.3] Manager review
If any line in a ticket has at least one flag, the whole ticket goes to the
employee's line manager with the status "Pending". The manager sees every line, its
receipt photo, its flags, and the trip details.

[POL-9.4] Manager decision on a ticket
The manager approves or rejects the whole ticket. A rejection must include:
  - a reason: missing evidence, insufficient evidence (for example a blurred photo),
    incorrect evidence, unclear expense purpose, policy concern, or other; and
  - a review comment of at least 10 characters explaining what is wrong.
An approval comment is optional but recommended when approving a flagged line. Once
recorded, the decision and comment are final and cannot be changed.

[POL-9.5] Decision history
Every decision on a trip or ticket, whether by a manager or by the system, is stored
permanently with the reviewer, decision, reason, comment and time. Decisions are
never overwritten or deleted.

[POL-9.6] AI has no role in approvals
Approval decisions are made only by these rules and by managers. Vera's answers are
never used to approve or reject a trip or an expense.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 10 - NON-REIMBURSABLE ITEMS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[POL-10.1] Items that are never reimbursed
Alcohol; personal entertainment, spa or gym charges; hotel minibar; traffic or
parking fines; flight or hotel upgrades paid personally; travel insurance bought
personally; costs of personal days added to a business trip; replacement of lost
personal items. Managers should reject tickets that include these items with the
reason "policy concern".

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 11 - CLAIM DEADLINE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[POL-11.1] Claim deadline
All expense tickets for a trip must be raised while the trip is "Approved - Active":
at the latest 90 days after the trip start date, or earlier if your manager completes
the trip after it ends. The trip page shows the automatic completion date.

SECTION 12 - WEATHER GUIDANCE FROM VERA
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[POL-12.1] How to get weather information
Weather is available only by asking Vera in the chat. Tell Vera the destination, the
start date and the end date, for example: "I am travelling to Bengaluru from 25
November to 28 November. What will the weather be?" If anything is missing or
unclear, Vera asks for it before checking. Vera does not take these details from your
trips; you always type them.

[POL-12.2] Weather source
Vera gets forecasts from the Open-Meteo public weather service. Every weather answer
shows the place Vera found, the source, the dates covered and the time the forecast
was retrieved. Vera uses only these values: weather condition, minimum and maximum
temperature, chance of rain, rain amount and maximum wind speed.

[POL-12.3] Forecast range
Open-Meteo forecasts cover today and the next 15 days (16 days in total). For dates
outside this range, or dates in the past, Vera says "Forecast not available" for those
dates and gives no temperatures, rain or wind values and no weather-specific packing
advice for them. Ask again closer to your travel date.

[POL-12.4] Packing and precaution advice
For dates with a forecast, Vera can suggest clothing, packing and precautions that
follow from the returned values, for example an umbrella when rain is forecast or
warm clothing when low temperatures are forecast. Vera never invents conditions or
warnings that the forecast does not show.

[POL-12.5] Weather advice is informational only
Weather guidance is a suggestion, not a safety assessment and not a travel approval.
Always check official government travel advisories and your client's site rules
before you travel.

SECTION 13 - PRIVACY AND USE OF THE AI ASSISTANT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[POL-13.1] Do not share sensitive personal data in chat
Do not type card numbers, bank details, PAN, Aadhaar, passport numbers or phone
numbers into the chat. TravelDesk masks such data automatically before storing it or
sending it to the AI model.

[POL-13.2] Chat sessions
Each time you open Vera, a new chat starts. Vera remembers the conversation only
within that chat. Closing the chat window ends it, and a chat also ends automatically
after 60 minutes without a message. A new chat starts with no memory of earlier
chats: give the destination and dates again if you need weather.

[POL-13.3] Chat history and records
Ended chats are kept. You can read your own past chats in Vera's "Past chats" list,
but you cannot continue them. Chats are stored for audit, troubleshooting and quality
review. Nobody else can open your chats in TravelDesk.

[POL-13.4] What Vera can and cannot do
Vera can answer policy and TravelDesk how-to questions and check the weather for a
destination and dates you provide. Vera cannot see your trips, tickets, expenses or
receipts, cannot see other employees' data, cannot approve or reject anything,
cannot submit or edit trips or tickets, cannot book travel, and cannot promise that
an expense will be reimbursed.

[POL-13.5] Where Vera is available
The Vera chat icon appears only on the employee pages "My Trips" and "Expense
Management". Vera is not available to managers.

SECTION 14 - TRAVELDESK PLATFORM GUIDE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[GUIDE-1] Signing in
Open TravelDesk and sign in with your company email and password. Your session lasts
60 minutes; after that, TravelDesk asks you to sign in again. Employees see "My
Trips", "Create Trip" and "Expense Management". Managers see "Approvals" and
"Spending Summary".

[GUIDE-2] Creating a travel request
Go to "Create Trip". Enter the destination, start date, end date and business
purpose, then click "Submit request". The new trip appears with its TRP ID and the
status "In Progress".

[GUIDE-3] Viewing your trips
Go to "My Trips". Every trip is listed with its ID, destination, dates, status and
automatic completion date. Use the filters "Upcoming", "Past" and "All". Click a trip
to open its details.

[GUIDE-4] Editing a trip before approval
Open a trip that is "In Progress" and click "Edit". Change the details and save.

[GUIDE-5] Expense Management
Go to "Expense Management" to see all your tickets for all trips, with their status.
From here you can raise a ticket for any "Approved - Active" trip, open a ticket, and
start a replacement for a rejected ticket.

[GUIDE-6] Raising an expense ticket
In Expense Management (or from an "Approved - Active" trip), click "Raise ticket" and
choose the trip. Add one line per expense: category, amount, expense date,
description, a photo of the receipt, and optionally the invoice number. Each photo
uploads as soon as you choose it, shows a preview and gets an EVD ID. Click "Add line"
for more lines, then "Submit ticket". "Submit ticket" stays disabled until every line
has a photo. TravelDesk then shows the ticket ID, an expense ID for each line, any
policy flags, and whether the ticket was auto-approved or sent to your manager.

[GUIDE-7] Adding forgotten expenses
If you missed an expense, raise another ticket on the same trip ([POL-5.5]).

[GUIDE-8] Tracking ticket status
In Expense Management or on the trip page, each ticket shows its status:
Auto-approved, Pending, Approved or Rejected. Open a ticket to see its lines, receipt
photos, flags, and the manager's decision, reason and comment.

[GUIDE-9] When a ticket is rejected
Open the rejected ticket and read the reason and comment. Click "Create new ticket".
TravelDesk opens a new, empty ticket for the same trip, linked to the rejected one.
Add the lines again and upload every receipt photo again, then submit ([POL-5.8]).
The rejected ticket cannot be edited or resubmitted.

[GUIDE-10] Completing a trip
Employees cannot complete a trip. When you have claimed everything, tell your
manager, who can mark the trip completed after it ends. Otherwise TravelDesk completes
it automatically 90 days after the trip start date ([POL-4.3]).

[GUIDE-11] Using Vera
On "My Trips" or "Expense Management", click the chat icon at the bottom right. Ask
about the expense policy, how to use TravelDesk, or the weather for a destination and
dates. Press Enter to send. Vera remembers what you said earlier in the same chat.
Click the X at the top of the window to end the chat; opening it again starts a new
chat. Use "Past chats" in the window to read your earlier chats.

[GUIDE-12] Managers: approving trip requests
Go to "Approvals" and open the "Trip requests" tab. Open a request, review the trip
details (employee, destination, dates, purpose), then click "Approve" or "Reject".
A rejection needs a review comment.

[GUIDE-13] Managers: reviewing expense tickets
Go to "Approvals" and open the "Expense tickets" tab. Each pending ticket shows its
lines, receipt photos and policy flags. Click "Approve" or "Reject". To reject, choose
a reason and write a review comment. Your decision and comment are final and appear
in the ticket history.

[GUIDE-14] Managers: completing trips
Go to "Approvals" and open the "Trips to complete" tab. It lists your team's active
trips whose end date has passed, with the number of tickets still pending and the
automatic completion date. Click "Mark completed" to close a trip.

[GUIDE-15] Managers: spending summary
Go to "Spending Summary" to see spending per trip, spending per category, and the
list of flagged expenses still waiting for a decision.

[GUIDE-16] Common messages and what to do
  "Incorrect email or password"   : check both and try again.
  "Session expired"               : sign in again (sessions last 60 minutes).
  "Trip is not active"            : tickets can only be raised on Approved - Active trips.
  "Trip is completed"             : no more tickets or expenses can be added to it.
  "Trip can no longer be edited"  : the manager has already decided on the request.
  "Expense date is before this trip was created" : change the date or claim it elsewhere.
  "Receipt photo required"        : add a photo to every line before submitting.
  "File type not allowed"         : upload a JPEG, PNG or WEBP photo.
  "File too large"                : the photo must be 5 MB or smaller.
  "Ticket is read-only"           : decided tickets cannot change; raise a new ticket.
  "Forecast not available"        : the dates are outside the 16-day forecast range.
  "Destination not found"         : check the spelling of the city name.
  "Which place did you mean?"     : add the state or country, e.g. "Hyderabad, India".
  "Weather service unavailable"   : try again later; nothing else is affected.
  "Assistant unavailable"         : the AI service is down; all other features work.
  "Chat ended"                    : a new chat starts automatically.

SECTION 15 - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[FAQ-1] My dinner cost INR 1,800. Will it be approved?
It is above the MEALS threshold of INR 1,500 ([POL-6.2]), so the ticket goes to your
manager for review. It is not rejected automatically. The manager decides.

[FAQ-2] I lost a receipt. Can I still claim?
Not in TravelDesk: every expense line needs a receipt photo ([POL-7.1]). Contact
Finance Operations about a lost receipt before raising a ticket ([POL-7.3]).

[FAQ-3] Can I claim my flight before the trip starts?
Yes, if the trip is "Approved - Active" and the flight was paid on or after the day
you created the trip request ([POL-8.2], [POL-4.2]).

[FAQ-4] My ticket was rejected. What now?
Read the reason and comment, then create a new ticket for the same trip with all lines
and receipt photos again ([POL-5.8], [GUIDE-9]). The rejected ticket cannot be edited.

[FAQ-5] Can I change a trip after it was approved?
No. Approved trips cannot be edited ([POL-3.5]). If your plans change, raise a new
travel request with the correct details and tell your manager which trip it replaces.

[FAQ-6] Why does Vera say "Forecast not available"?
Your dates are outside the forecast range of today plus 15 days ([POL-12.3]). Ask
again closer to your travel date.

[FAQ-7] Can Vera approve my expense or check my ticket status?
No. Vera cannot see your tickets and cannot approve anything ([POL-9.6], [POL-13.4]).
Check the status in Expense Management.

[FAQ-8] Is a trip of 35 days allowed?
No. The maximum trip length is 30 days ([POL-3.2]). Split it into two requests.

[FAQ-9] Can I mark my own trip as completed?
No. Only your line manager can complete a trip, or TravelDesk completes it 90 days
after the trip start date ([POL-4.3]).

[FAQ-10] How long do I have to claim my expenses?
While the trip is "Approved - Active": at the latest until 90 days after the trip
start date, or until your manager completes the trip ([POL-11.1]).

[FAQ-11] Can I request a trip for next year?
Yes, if it starts no more than one year (365 days) from today ([POL-3.2]).

[FAQ-12] Does Vera remember my last chat?
No. Each chat starts fresh ([POL-13.2]). You can read old chats in "Past chats".
